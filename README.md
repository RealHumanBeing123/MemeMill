# MemeMill

An offline meme and caption maker, in the same spirit as cURL Craft. The HTML file is all that you need.

Open a picture, add captions and stickers, and save the result. Nothing is uploaded: the page makes no network requests, and your images never leave your computer.

What you can do:
- Pictures: open, drag and drop, or paste (Ctrl+V) an image. With no picture you get a blank canvas.
- Shape and crop: keep the original shape or choose square, portrait, story, 4:3 or 16:9, then slide the crop into place.
- Edit the picture: rotate, mirror, brightness, contrast, colour, black and white, and blur.
- Text: add as many text layers as you like. Choose the font (Impact is the default), size, colours, outline, shadow, bold, italic, capitals, alignment, wrapping, rotation and opacity.
- Stickers: 64 built-in emoji, any emoji you type or paste, and your own pictures or logos placed on top.
- Layers: click to select, drag to move, use the corner dot to resize and the top dot to rotate. Reorder, hide, duplicate or delete layers from the list.
- Bars and border: add a white caption bar above or below the picture (the classic "caption" meme style), plus a border.
- Layout presets: classic top and bottom text, top only, bottom only, caption bar on top, caption bar on the bottom.
- Undo and redo: Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y, with the buttons as well.
- Save: PNG, JPEG or WebP, at the editor size or 2048, 1080, 720 or 512 px wide. Or copy the image to your clipboard.

Keyboard shortcuts (with a layer selected): Delete removes it, arrow keys nudge it (Shift for bigger steps), Escape deselects.

Good to know:
- Fonts come from your computer, so a font may look a little different on another device. Impact ships with Windows and macOS.
- Animated GIFs are saved as their first frame.
- Very large pictures are scaled down so the longest side is at most 2400 px.
- Only share images you have the right to use.

Just open `MemeMill.html` in any browser. It works with no internet connection.
